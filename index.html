The Manifesto

This project was born from a desire for "another truth."

Traditional film history is often a singular epic written by men—one where the female experience is simplified, romanticized, or erased.

This is not merely a list; it is an archaeology of perspective. I celebrate cinema that champions Her Gaze—not simply as a matter of gender, but as a revolutionary cinematic language.

Here, we reject the stereotype. We embrace the broken, the fluid, and the unruly.
