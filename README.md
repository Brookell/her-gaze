# HER GAZE
### Reclaiming the Perspective. A Cinema of Her Own.

![Project Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Active-gold?style=flat-square)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Personal-black?style=flat-square)

**HER GAZE** is a cinematic portfolio and digital archive dedicated to exploring film history through the lens of female experience and revolutionary cinematic language. Born from a desire for "another truth," this project rejects singular, male-centric narratives in favor of the messy, the fluid, and the unruly.

---

## 📽️ The Mission
This project is not merely a list; it is an archaeology of perspective. We celebrate cinema that champions *Her Gaze*—not simply as a matter of gender, but as a fundamental shift in how we observe and are observed.

## 🎞️ Curated Themes
The archive is organized into high-impact thematic collections:
- **Maternal Bonds**: The origin and the odyssey.
- **The Unruly Woman**: Not here to be liked, but to be seen.
- **Domestic Battlegrounds**: The hidden frontlines of subtle resistance.
- **Reclaiming the Body**: Moving from passive object to active subject.

## 🛠️ Technology
Built with a focus on premium editorial aesthetics and smooth interactivity:
- **Core**: Semantic HTML5 & Vanilla JavaScript.
- **Aesthetics**: Custom Modern CSS with a focus on cinematic spacing and glassmorphism.
- **Data**: Integrated with a local JSON registry and support for Airtable API.
- **UX**: Scroll-triggered reveals, interactive modals, and responsive grid layouts.

## 📬 From the Creator
> "This space is a personal archive, a curated collection of films and moments that have shaped how I see the world. It is an invitation to look through my lens, and I hope you find something here that resonates with you."
